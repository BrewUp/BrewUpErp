# BrewUp Brewery Operations Guide

## Company Overview

BrewUp is a craft brewery specialized in the production and distribution of premium beers.

The company manages the entire lifecycle of its products, from recipe design and production planning to warehouse management, sales, and customer delivery.

The mission of BrewUp is to consistently deliver high-quality beer while maintaining efficient business operations and excellent customer service.

---

## Organizational Structure

BrewUp is organized into several business areas.

### Master Data

Master Data manages the core reference information used throughout the company.

Responsibilities include:

* Beer catalog management
* Customer management
* Supplier management
* Product definitions
* Beer styles and characteristics

Master Data acts as the source of truth for business identities and reference information.

---

### Sales

The Sales department manages customer demand and commercial activities.

Responsibilities include:

* Creating sales orders
* Managing customer relationships
* Tracking order status
* Monitoring customer demand
* Managing commercial commitments

Sales is responsible for understanding what customers want to buy.

---

### Warehouse

The Warehouse department manages physical inventory and shipment preparation.

Responsibilities include:

* Stock management
* Inventory monitoring
* Shipment preparation
* Reorder threshold management
* Dispatch operations

Warehouse is responsible for ensuring products are available when needed.

---

### Production

Production is responsible for brewing and packaging beer.

Responsibilities include:

* Brewing operations
* Recipe execution
* Fermentation monitoring
* Packaging activities
* Quality control

Production transforms raw ingredients into finished products.

---

## Product Portfolio

BrewUp currently produces several beer styles.

Examples include:

* IPA (India Pale Ale)
* Weissbier
* Pilsner
* Stout
* Amber Ale

Each beer style has unique characteristics, ingredients, and production requirements.

---

## Customer Types

BrewUp serves multiple customer segments.

Examples include:

### Retail Customers

Independent stores and specialty beer shops.

### Restaurants

Restaurants offering local and craft beer selections.

### Bars and Pubs

Hospitality businesses requiring regular beer deliveries.

### Distributors

Organizations responsible for distributing BrewUp products to larger markets.

---

## Sales Order Lifecycle

A typical sales order follows several stages.

1. Order Creation
2. Order Validation
3. Inventory Verification
4. Shipment Preparation
5. Dispatch
6. Order Completion

Different departments collaborate during this process.

Sales manages customer communication.

Warehouse manages fulfillment.

Production may become involved when inventory replenishment is required.

---

## Inventory Management Principles

BrewUp follows several inventory management principles.

* Maintain sufficient stock for customer demand.
* Monitor reorder thresholds continuously.
* Reduce product shortages.
* Avoid excessive inventory accumulation.
* Prioritize product freshness.

Inventory decisions are based on both historical demand and current business conditions.

---

## Quality Standards

Quality is a critical aspect of BrewUp operations.

The company continuously monitors:

* Ingredient quality
* Brewing consistency
* Fermentation conditions
* Packaging quality
* Customer feedback

All departments contribute to maintaining product quality.

---

## Business Objectives

The primary objectives of BrewUp are:

* Deliver high-quality beer
* Satisfy customer demand
* Maintain operational efficiency
* Minimize waste
* Improve inventory management
* Support sustainable business growth

All business functions contribute to achieving these objectives.

---

## Operational Collaboration

Successful operations require collaboration between departments.

Master Data provides business reference information.

Sales provides customer demand signals.

Warehouse manages stock and fulfillment.

Production creates finished products.

Together these business areas enable BrewUp to deliver beer efficiently and consistently to its customers.
