# BrewUp Inventory Reorder Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that BrewUp maintains sufficient inventory levels to satisfy customer demand while minimizing excess stock and storage costs.

This policy applies to all finished beer products managed by the Warehouse department.

---

## Inventory Monitoring

Inventory levels are continuously monitored through the BrewUp ERP system.

Each product has a predefined reorder threshold.

When the available inventory falls below the threshold, the product becomes eligible for replenishment.

Warehouse personnel are responsible for reviewing inventory alerts and coordinating with Production when replenishment is required.

---

## Reorder Threshold Calculation

Reorder thresholds are based on:

* Historical sales volume
* Average weekly demand
* Production lead time
* Seasonal demand fluctuations
* Strategic business priorities

Thresholds are reviewed quarterly.

---

## IPA Reorder Policy

### Product

IPA (India Pale Ale)

### Reorder Threshold

500 bottles

### Target Inventory Level

1,500 bottles

### Critical Inventory Level

250 bottles

---

## Standard Replenishment Process

When IPA inventory falls below 500 bottles:

1. A reorder recommendation is generated.
2. Warehouse personnel verify current stock.
3. Production planning is notified.
4. A new production batch is scheduled if required.

The objective is to restore inventory to approximately 1,500 bottles.

---

## Critical Stock Scenario

When IPA inventory falls below 250 bottles:

* The situation is considered critical.
* Production receives high-priority notification.
* New customer orders may require additional review.
* Warehouse management must assess potential stock shortages.

Critical inventory situations should be resolved within five business days whenever possible.

---

## Large Order Assessment

Large customer orders can create inventory risk.

Any order that would consume more than 20% of the current IPA inventory must be reviewed before confirmation.

The review must consider:

* Current stock availability
* Existing open orders
* Planned production batches
* Expected deliveries

---

## Production Coordination

Production and Warehouse teams collaborate to maintain inventory levels.

Production planning should prioritize IPA replenishment when:

* Inventory falls below the reorder threshold.
* Demand trends indicate increased consumption.
* Multiple large customer orders are pending.

---

## Business Objective

The primary objective of this policy is to:

* Prevent stock shortages.
* Support customer satisfaction.
* Maintain predictable production schedules.
* Reduce emergency production requests.
* Improve inventory planning accuracy.

All inventory decisions should balance customer demand, production capacity, and operational efficiency.
